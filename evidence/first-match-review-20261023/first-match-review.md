## One combat-first HUD proposal (evidence-only B)

The A and B pairs are separate 1440×900 and 844×390 files under `ab/`. A is the real captured HUD; B is a static image re-composition of the same live combat scene and canonical HUD/art pixels, made outside application source. No gameplay event, ability, instruction, weapon, icon, or hero was invented. The desktop/mobile captures are from one uninterrupted BOT match; their live scene clocks are 3.729 s (desktop) and 3.828 s (mobile), a 99 ms difference. Each B keeps its corresponding viewport’s live identity/skill-card artwork; the mobile arena/top strip are paired from the desktop capture to avoid carrying the temporary pause banner into the proposal.

- **Desktop B:** keep the canonical HP/timer header; center the actual square arena; move P1 identity and the two real J/K cards into a dedicated left rail; keep P2 identity/skill state/radar and weapon strip in a right rail. No new movement or aim hints.
- **Landscape mobile B:** retain HP/timer at top; reserve the left lane for the real arena; group P1 identity, the same J/K cards, and current weapon/unarmed strip in the right lane. The 350 px arena and card scale are explicit proposal measurements, not an app CSS build.
- **Preserved:** Newbot hero art, weapon/skill card art, Gold palette, live fighter pose/VFX, health/timer values, weapon state, and existing J/K labels. No new ability or touch-only claim.
- **Self-critique / trade-offs:** Desktop arena is reduced from the live ~836 px square to 740 px to open rails; that may reduce combat visibility. The mobile composition increases arena width from about 340 to 350 px but the rail remains dense and is **not touch-tested**. This single Newbot screenshot does not validate all 14 hero names, card lengths, or hero-specific VFX. B omits the temporary coach card rather than inventing a new, smaller one; a non-obscuring coach placement is an unresolved design decision. B is a conversation prototype only and must not be translated into source until the owner approves/rejects it.
