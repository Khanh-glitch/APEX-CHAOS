The OFL notices ship in `node_modules/@fontsource/*/LICENSE`.

**Art and audio.** No third-party sprites, tiles or sound files are used. Every sprite, tile texture, icon and VFX is original pixel art generated by `src/game/art.ts` and `src/game/render.ts`. Every sound is synthesized at runtime by `src/game/audio.ts`.

**Candidates considered but not adopted.** NinjaAdventure, Kenney, simplex-noise, easystar and idb were not used. Instead, the build has its own seeded value noise, A* for roads and creatures, and a raw IndexedDB wrapper. That was enough for the needs here and kept the art style in one consistent hand.
