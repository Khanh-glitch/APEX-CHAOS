## In-scope invariants (not translation defects)

- `APEX CHAOS`, canonical fighter/weapon/skin/event names, `SCRAP BASIN`, `NEWBOT`, P1/P2, BOT/LOCAL, AC, A1/A2 and technical IDs stay canonical where used as product identities.
- Home event branding (`SPECIAL EVENT`, `BOT WAVE`, `BOXE / ARSENAL SURVIVAL`) must not be reworded or restyled without owner approval.
- Gold frame, font roles, layout, gameplay and economy remain unchanged by the locale work. Vietnamese rendering retains the bundled `ApexVietCondensed` face; the glyph/browser test covers precomposed upper/lower marks and decomposed combining sequences.
- Chromium device metrics are emulation only. Physical Safari/iOS and Android behavior is not claimed.
