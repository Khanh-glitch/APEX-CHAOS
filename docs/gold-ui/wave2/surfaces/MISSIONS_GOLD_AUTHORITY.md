# APEX CHAOS — Missions Gold Product Authority

Status: PRODUCT/UX LOCKED — VISUAL GOLD PENDING — PUBLIC SURFACE REMAINS LOCKED.

## Purpose

Missions provide finite, meaningful secondary objectives around the combat systems that already exist.

They do not create a second Story and do not turn APEX CHAOS into a checklist live-service.

## Internal identity

Home label: **MISSIONS**.

Internal visual identity: **OPERATIONS CONSOLE**.

Neutral combat telemetry / objective terminal; do not invent a commander, faction or contract employer.

## V1 categories

Only:
- FIELD OBJECTIVES
- FIGHTER OBJECTIVES

No Daily / Weekly taxonomy in V1.

## Explicitly not authorized

No:
- login streak;
- battle pass;
- season timer;
- daily reset;
- weekly reset;
- mission token;
- “spend AC” mission;
- “use Lucky Draw N times” mission;
- “buy a fighter” mission.

## Objective quality law

A good objective rewards meaningful outcomes.

Good:
- successfully trap an opponent;
- confirm a disarm;
- convert a floor weapon;
- redirect a relevant projectile/object;
- use multiple real floor weapons in a battle.

Bad:
- press A1 20 times;
- cast a skill 10 times;
- log in;
- spend currency.

Do not instrument VFX visibility as gameplay truth.

Fighter Objectives must listen to canonical gameplay outcomes/events.

## Eligibility

Fighter Objectives only appear for owned fighters.

Do not show a locked fighter mission as a Shop funnel.

Economic mission authority must be designed against farmability. Local 1v1 cannot automatically become a trustworthy economic reward source merely because it is playable.

## Active objective count

Keep the surface small:
- approximately 2–3 Field Objectives;
- at most one contextual/tracked Fighter Objective in early V1.

Avoid a wall of 15 tasks and notification debt.

## Rewards

Use existing value domains only when authority exists.

Initial likely economic reward: AC by config.

Mastery reward may be introduced only after Mastery authority exists.

No new mission currency.

Completion reward should settle automatically once canonical authority confirms completion; avoid a generic “Claim” chore unless a future system genuinely needs deferred claiming.

## Battle HUD integration

Battle remains combat-first.

At most one intentionally tracked objective may use a tiny HUD readout.

Do not show giant progress toasts during combat.

Completion feedback is preferably surfaced in Result/post-match.

## Visual hierarchy

Future prototype:
- objective rail/index;
- one selected operation detail;
- progress;
- condition;
- reward projection;
- fighter avatar/skill icon reuse when needed.

No paper contract-board cliché is required.

## Audio

Theme suppressed while Missions is active, preserving playhead.

Use restrained terminal/mechanical SFX/ambience; no dedicated track required in V1.

## Data boundary

Mission definitions should be data-driven but use a controlled evaluator vocabulary, not a generic scripting engine.

Minimal progress concept:
- missionId;
- revision;
- progress;
- completedAt;
- rewardedAt.

Client presentation must not award economic currency during render.

## Prohibited drift

No live-service cadence without product authority.
No fake Story.
No generic grind tasks.
No mechanic inference from animation.
No cross-sell objectives.
