# APEX CHAOS — Home HUD research and visual acceptance (2026-10-28)

## Scope and boundaries

Compared **58 individually identifiable game menu/lobby/home/hub reference screens** retrieved in image research. These are *not all 58 home screens*: related character-select, hub, and title interfaces are explicitly marked below. Each is a visual reference, not a copied art asset. The production APEX Gold shell and all fighter art remain authoritative.

## Findings applied to APEX

1. **Art-first hierarchy:** Hero silhouette and Scrap Basin narrative are composition anchors. Avoid a persistent second black panel across the Quest title or event card. Strong references: Armored Core VI, Hades, Battlefield 6, Apex Legends, The Finals.
2. **Currency is passive and always visible:** One tiny AC chip at the top. No second avatar tile, unknown '?' glyph or test-balance sticker. Strong references: Zenless Zone Zero, Arena of Valor, Honor of Kings, NIKKE, Rocket League.
3. **Secondary controls live behind intent:** An unobtrusive, tactile settings glyph beside the existing coin reveals the language and Play Guide; nothing floats in the default Home frame. Strong references: Armored Core VI, Cyberpunk 2077, Hearthstone, Elden Ring Nightreign.
4. **No more rectangles for every action:** Hero, Free Battle CTA and 7-item native bottom nav remain; one compact cinematic-styled popover is used only on demand.
5. **Touch and accessibility remain real:** Settings click target ≥36px visual (Gold scale), keyboard/ESC close, screen-reader labels, full ENG/VIE choice and functional Help. The 350 AC authority is unaffected.
6. **Evidence must include visual critique:** Camera-stable screenshot with drawer CLOSED at 522×320, 844×390, 390×844 and 1440×900; check title clearance, negative space and coin hierarchy. Also screenshot drawer OPEN and verify interactions. Automated overlap tests are necessary but never sufficient for aesthetic approval.

## Reject criteria

- Persistent LANG / ENG / VIE / HELP boxes remain over the scene.
- Detached or oversized '?' icon on the currency.
- Quest headline, event artwork, Free Battle CTA or Gold logo obscured.
- Palette shifts away from APEX amber/charcoal/metal; Gold source files modified rather than layered.
- Open panel extends beyond safe area, cannot be escaped, or changes currency during a language switch.
- User can accidentally select blue text by swiping HUD.
