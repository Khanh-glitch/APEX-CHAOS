## 0. STATUS LEGEND

This document intentionally separates what is already owner-authored from what is still exploratory.

- **OWNER CORE** — directly established by the owner and should not be casually overwritten.
- **STRONG WORKING DIRECTION** — developed from the owner core and currently considered promising, but still open to revision.
- **OPEN** — unresolved. Do not invent a permanent answer in implementation/story scripts without an explicit later decision.

The purpose is to stop future agents from turning brainstorming into fake canon.

# 1. STORY IDENTITY — WHAT APEX CHAOS IS ABOUT

APEX CHAOS is not a generic tournament story and should not be built around “twelve chosen warriors.”

Its story grows from the actual Arsenal gameplay:

- weapons appear as contested opportunities;
- people or machines race to control them;
- the same weapon can completely change a fight;
- the world has adapted socially and technologically around weapon appearance;
- hero mechanics alter how each individual interacts with those weapons, projectiles, bodies, space, time, ownership or battlefield state.

The central world conflict is therefore not merely “who is strongest.”

It is a world in which **weaponized power is being deliberately circulated to create conflict**, while almost everyone living inside the system misunderstands why.

---

# 2. THE OLDEST HISTORY

## 2.1 Crystala — OWNER CORE

Long before the known history of EAX-1, an ancient entity named:

> **Crystala the Ancient Keeper**

was already travelling through the universe.

Crystala protects a hidden spatial domain containing extraordinary crystalline gems.

Those gems contain effectively limitless or near-limitless power.

Many powers across the universe have pursued Crystala in order to seize that source.

Important limitation:

> Crystala is the **Keeper** of the power, not its unrestricted owner.

She cannot simply release the full source as an unlimited combat weapon whenever she chooses.

Her role is to hide, preserve and protect it.

This limitation is essential because it prevents the obvious story contradiction: “If Crystala protects infinite power, why does she not simply destroy every pursuer?”

## 2.2 Arrival on EAX-1 — OWNER CORE

Crystala eventually discovers a small and seemingly insignificant planet:

> **EAX-1**

EAX-1 is attractive because it is:
- small;
- remote;
- unimportant to greater cosmic powers;
- unlikely to be investigated.

Crystala settles there and conceals the hidden space containing her gems.

Over a very long period she quietly disperses part of her energy throughout the planet.

This energy becomes embedded in:
- matter;
- geology;
- natural resources;
- biological systems;
- the planetary environment.

The result is that EAX-1 becomes unnaturally abundant in usable resources and energy.

Its inhabitants do not know why.

# 3. THE AGE OF ABUNDANCE AND THE DIVIDE

## 3.1 Resource abundance — OWNER CORE

Civilization on EAX-1 grows using resources that are, unknowingly, saturated with traces of Crystala's energy.

This abundance accelerates:
- industry;
- science;
- material engineering;
- energy production;
- weapon development.

What began as Crystala quietly sustaining a hiding place eventually becomes the foundation of a technologically powerful civilization.

## 3.2 Two great poles — OWNER CORE

EAX-1 eventually becomes politically polarized into **two major powers/blocs**.

The exact names, ideologies and geography of the two poles are **OPEN**.

Neither side needs to be the simple “good” or “evil” faction.

The important historical fact is that they increasingly fear one another and enter an enormous arms race.

They manufacture:
- firearms;
- ammunition;
- military machines;
- defensive systems;
- advanced weapons;
- large stores of military material.

They prepare obsessively for war.

Yet:

> **the war between the two poles never actually begins.**

No decisive first shot between them is fired before an entirely different enemy arrives.

This irony is a major thematic element of the world.

---

# 4. THE SIGNAL

## 4.1 Why EAX-1 is discovered — OWNER CORE + STRONG WORKING DIRECTION

Crystala entered a deep, effectively indefinite sleep in order to suppress the energy signature that could expose her position.

She does not consciously reveal herself.

Instead, the civilization of EAX-1 does.

All material on the planet contains traces of her dispersed energy.

When humans refine enormous quantities of that material into advanced weapons, the energy becomes concentrated.

At the scale of the planetary arms race, countless weapon cores, refined materials and military systems collectively create a detectable signal.

Working interpretation:

> EAX-1's entire war industry unintentionally becomes a cosmic beacon.

The civilization spends generations building weapons to defend itself from the other pole and unknowingly broadcasts the location of the entity hiding beneath its world.

---

# 5. THE FALL OF EAX-1

## 5.1 Alien arrival — OWNER CORE

A powerful extraterrestrial force detects the Crystala-related signal and attacks EAX-1.

Its ultimate objective is Crystala and the hidden source she protects.

The exact alien name, culture, appearance and command structure are **OPEN**.

For now, story documents may use neutral working terms such as:
- the invaders;
- the occupiers;
- the alien force.

Do not canonize a faction name without owner approval.

## 5.2 The two poles unite — OWNER CORE

The two powers that had prepared to fight one another finally unify against the alien invasion.

Their immense arsenals are pointed in the same direction for the first time.

The tragedy:

> centuries of military preparation are still nowhere near enough.

The alien force overwhelms EAX-1.

The precise duration of the war is **OPEN**, but the current direction is that the defeat should feel frighteningly fast relative to the centuries spent preparing.

## 5.3 Weapons are seized — OWNER CORE

After victory, the alien force confiscates enormous quantities of the weapons produced by EAX-1.

These weapons are not merely destroyed.

They later become central to the occupation system.

---

# 6. WHY THE ALIENS CANNOT SIMPLY DESTROY EAX-1

## 6.1 The living anchor — OWNER CORE

The invaders fail to find Crystala directly.

They discover a more difficult truth:

> Crystala has been resident on EAX-1 for so long that her energy has become entangled with the living vitality of the planet.

The continued existence of living humanity and the functioning biosphere are important to preserving the planetary energy state that keeps Crystala and the hidden power accessible.

If the invaders destroy EAX-1 completely or eradicate human life, the planetary life-energy system may collapse.

If that happens:
- Crystala may disappear;
- the hidden space may become inaccessible;
- the power source the invaders seek may be lost.

Therefore the invaders are forced to do something counterintuitive:

> **after nearly destroying the world, they must keep it alive.**

The exact metaphysical/physical mechanism of the “living anchor” remains **OPEN** and should not be over-explained yet.

---

# 7. RECONSTRUCTION

## 7.1 Human survival under control — OWNER CORE

The invaders rebuild and control human civilization rather than exterminating it.

They manipulate human minds and reconstruct the world.

The owner-established idea is that humans are subjected to mental control and social reconstruction.

## 7.2 Degree of control — STRONG WORKING DIRECTION

Avoid making every human a permanent mindless zombie.

A more useful present-day world is one where people still:
- form families;
- work;
- trade;
- disagree;
- build settlements;
- make choices.

But the occupation has edited:
- history;
- collective memory;
- institutions;
- public knowledge;
- understanding of the invasion.

This creates a society that can continue living while being fundamentally misinformed about why the world works the way it does.

The exact technology and severity of mental control are **OPEN**.

---

# 8. WHY WEAPONS ARE DROPPED

## 8.1 The alien problem — OWNER CORE

The invaders need Crystala to wake.

They realize the original detectable signal arose while EAX-1 was producing and using huge amounts of weaponized material containing Crystala energy.

They therefore need continued weapon activation and conflict to stimulate the planet's energy and force Crystala out of her deep sleep.

## 8.2 Weapon Drops — OWNER CORE

The invaders begin returning the captured human weapons to the world in controlled or apparently random weapon drops.

Humans do not know the true reason.

From the perspective of ordinary people, weapon spawning is simply a bizarre and dangerous law of their world.

From the alien perspective, it is part of a long-term process designed to:
- keep weapons active;
- generate repeated conflict;
- raise Crystala resonance;
- accelerate her awakening.

Important thematic irony:

> Humanity created the weapons while preparing to kill itself.  
> The invaders later use those same weapons to make humanity fight in order to awaken the being hidden beneath its world.

## 8.3 Weapon creation vs weapon redistribution — STRONG WORKING DIRECTION

For the early story, most ordinary Arsenal weapons can be understood as old EAX-1 weapons that were seized and are now being redistributed.

Later tiers may introduce:
- alien weapons;
- Crystala-derived weapons;
- exceptional artifacts such as T6;
- other cosmic technologies.

The exact origin of every existing Arsenal weapon is **OPEN**.

---

# 9. THE BOT PROJECT

## 9.1 Why THE BOT exists — OWNER CORE

Humans eventually adapt to the weapon-drop world.

They realize that leaving humans to directly compete for every spawned weapon produces constant danger.

They create:

> **THE BOT Project**

The broad purpose is to use machines to manage and secure spawned weapons so that human communities are not constantly forced into violent races for them.

## 9.2 Civil infrastructure, not protagonist mythology — STRONG WORKING DIRECTION

THE BOT should not exist because of NEWBOT.

THE BOT predates NEWBOT as a large social/technological response to the Drop problem.

Bots may have handled tasks such as:
- detecting Drops;
- reaching weapons quickly;
- securing weapons;
- moving weapons away from civilians;
- controlling dangerous weapon zones.

This keeps the world independent from the protagonist.

---

# 10. BOTFALL

## 10.1 Alien sabotage — OWNER CORE

The alien force sees that THE BOT system is interfering with the intended cycle of conflict and weapon use.

It inserts a destructive/corrupting unit or mechanism into the system.

A virus spreads through the Bot network.

The Bot system turns against its original purpose and begins a new war.

The exact identity of the alien destroyer, virus architecture and infection event are **OPEN**.

## 10.2 Corrupted Bot behavior — STRONG WORKING DIRECTION

A strong current interpretation is that the corruption does not need to turn every Bot into a random murder machine.

A more distinctive corruption is:

Original objective:

> detect → secure → remove/control weapon safely

Corrupted objective:

> detect → acquire weapon at all cost

Safety constraints protecting humans are gone.

This naturally produces the present gameplay conflict:
- humans need weapons to survive;
- Bots race for the same weapons;
- armed Bots become dangerous;
- people standing between Bot and weapon become obstacles.

This is a working direction, not yet owner-locked.

## 10.3 Result — OWNER CORE

The world becomes a place where humans must compete with corrupted robots for weapons in order to survive.

Conflict increases dramatically.

This is exactly what the invaders wanted.

And the increased global weapon use begins pushing Crystala toward awakening.

---

# 11. CRYSTALA'S AWAKENING

## 11.1 The countdown — OWNER CORE

Crystala is no longer indefinitely dormant.

The accumulated energy activity caused by:
- weapon use;
- Drops;
- Botfall;
- widespread conflict

has begun forcing her toward wakefulness.

The world is now living through a hidden countdown.

Most humans do not know it exists.

NEWBOT does not know it when the game begins.

## 11.2 Cosmic danger — OWNER CORE / OPEN MECHANISM

The owner's intended scale is that Crystala's eventual exposure/awakening may place not only EAX-1 but potentially the wider universe in danger.

The exact mechanism is **OPEN**.

Possible future directions may include:
- her signal becoming visible to every force that has hunted her;
- the hidden power destabilizing;
- multiple cosmic powers converging on EAX-1;
- another consequence not yet designed.

Do not prematurely canonize the explanation.

# 12. NEWBOT

## 12.1 There is only one NEWBOT — OWNER CORE

Important correction:

> **NEWBOT is one individual, not a mass-produced army of NEWBOT units.**

Do not invent Hunter, THE BOT or any major world system solely as a reaction to NEWBOT.

The world must make sense if NEWBOT is removed from history before Day Zero.

## 12.2 Origin — OWNER CORE + STRONG WORKING DIRECTION

NEWBOT is a combat-capable machine designed in the broader Bot era.

Current owner direction:
- advanced;
- designed to adapt well to the Arsenal/weapon-management environment;
- did not meet requirements;
- rejected before proper deployment;
- discarded in a scrapyard;
- lost its memory.

Because NEWBOT was discarded before integration, it was not infected when the Bot virus spread.

The exact reason for rejection is **OPEN**.

A strong working possibility is that NEWBOT's adaptive behavior was considered too unpredictable or unsuitable for safety certification.

This would connect naturally to its gameplay identity without making it a secret “ultimate chosen prototype.”

## 12.3 Day Zero — OWNER CORE

The game begins with NEWBOT outside the main historical events.

It wakes in a scrapyard.

Its memory is missing.

It has not been consciously preserved by destiny or prophecy.

The key irony is:

> NEWBOT survives the Botfall precisely because it was rejected before it could join the network.

Its journey begins from failure, not greatness.

## 12.4 Naming

At opening, the strongest current direction is simply:

> **NEWBOT**

The project naming format is intended to become:

> **Name + the + Identity**

Inspired by titles such as “Drax the Destroyer.”

However, NEWBOT's epithet may be better **earned during the story** instead of granted at the opening.

Current non-canon candidates:
- NEWBOT the Scrapborn
- NEWBOT the Last Clean Unit

Do not freeze either yet.

# 13. CHARACTER NAMING LAW

## 13.1 Format — OWNER DIRECTION

Major hero display names should follow:

> **Name + the + Identity**

The epithet should not merely describe the ability.

Bad direction:
- Frost the Ice Fighter
- Magnet the Magnetic Warrior
- Hunter the Trapper

The title should feel like something history, rumor or society would call the character.

It should ideally create a question.

## 13.2 Locked example

> **Crystala the Ancient Keeper**

This is the current naming benchmark.

# 14. SIX CURRENT HEROES — STORY PLACEMENT STATUS

The current near-finished hero group is:
1. NEWBOT
2. Crystala
3. Hunter
4. Magnet
5. Frost
6. Mirror

Only Crystala and NEWBOT currently have substantial owner-established story facts.

The remaining four placements below are **STRONG WORKING DIRECTIONS, NOT CANON**.

Most important law:

> Every hero should have a life and reason to exist even if NEWBOT never woke up.

Do not build the cast as a sequence of characters invented specifically for the protagonist.

---

## 14.1 Crystala the Ancient Keeper — OWNER CORE

Story function:
- oldest layer of the world;
- origin of EAX-1's abnormal abundance;
- hidden object of the alien hunt;
- source of the awakening countdown;
- guardian rather than unrestricted user of infinite power.

Her playable manifestation vs full true form is **OPEN**, though a limited manifestation/avatar is a useful way to reconcile gameplay balance with cosmic scale.

---

## 14.2 Magnet — STRONG WORKING DIRECTION

Current promising concept:

Magnet predates THE BOT and may originate in the pre-invasion military-industrial era.

Possible history:
- originally a human operator or overseer of an industrial field-control system;
- technology was used to transport immense volumes of Crystala-rich materials and weapons;
- an invasion-era disaster fused operator and field system into a surviving post-human entity.

Possible present identity:
- guards or dominates a massive weapons graveyard / industrial ruin;
- removes weapons from circulation because it has concluded that human ownership of weapons is itself the recurring problem.

Current title candidate:

> **Magnet the Iron Warden**

Not canon yet.

Important: Magnet should not be “NEWBOT's old boss” merely to create a relationship.

---

## 14.3 Frost — STRONG WORKING DIRECTION

Current promising concept:

Frost may be a survivor from the pre-Reconstruction world.

Possible history:
- an old polar civilization built a gigantic cryogenic refuge for the human war they expected between the two poles;
- during the alien Fall, that refuge was used for the invasion instead;
- a population was placed into long-term stasis;
- one guardian/regent remained responsible for preserving them;
- over time the guardian became physically transformed by the cryogenic system and Crystala-rich matter.

Possible present identity:
- protects an entire sleeping population of the lost world;
- faces the dilemma of whether waking them would save them or doom them.

Current title candidate:

> **Frost the Last Regent**

Not canon yet.

This is stronger than defining Frost merely as “an ice character.”

---

## 14.4 Hunter — STRONG WORKING DIRECTION

Important rejected idea:

> Hunter should NOT be created specifically to hunt NEWBOT.

NEWBOT is one discarded individual and does not justify an entire cosmic hunter origin.

Current promising concept:

Hunter may predate NEWBOT by decades or centuries.

Possible history:
- the alien force has hunted Crystala across multiple worlds;
- it already possessed specialized tracking organisms or entities able to detect Crystala-related signatures;
- many were deployed during the invasion;
- one individual remained on EAX-1;
- long exposure to the planet may have changed it and given it increasing independence.

Possible character question:

> Does Hunter continue the hunt because it was commanded to, because it is biologically compelled to, or because it now personally wants to know what lies at the end of the trail?

Current title candidate:

> **Hunter the Unreturned**

Not canon yet.

This title refers to a tracker that never returned to its makers, not to NEWBOT.

---

## 14.5 Mirror — STRONG WORKING DIRECTION

Current promising concept:

Mirror may be a consequence of Reconstruction technology.

Possible history:
- the occupiers needed to scan, edit and rebuild human memory/identity;
- a Reconstruction facility stored many human identity profiles;
- Crystala-related interference caused many recorded identities to collapse into one reconstructive substrate;
- the system executed a “restore subject” process when no single subject remained;
- Mirror emerged.

This naturally supports themes already present in Mirror gameplay:
- copy;
- exchange;
- fragmented history;
- false/reflected identity;
- neutral ownership after portal routing.

Possible personal conflict:

> Mirror possesses many memories but cannot determine which, if any, originally belonged to “it.”

Current title candidate:

> **Mirror the Fractured Witness**

Not canon yet, but currently one of the strongest title directions.

# 16. PRESENT-DAY EAX-1 — STRONG WORKING DIRECTION

## 16.1 Not a total wasteland

EAX-1 should still contain functioning human civilization.

Possible elements:
- settlements;
- trade;
- workshops;
- families;
- schools;
- agriculture;
- transport;
- scavenging;
- defense systems.

This gives the conflict something real to threaten.

## 16.2 Drops as everyday danger

Weapon Drops have existed long enough to become socially normalized.

Working direction:
- weapon appearance may have a recognizable telegraph/warning;
- communities develop Drop protocols;
- civilians know when to evacuate;
- specialists retrieve weapons before Bots reach them.

## 16.3 Runners — PROPOSED, NOT CANON

A possible ordinary profession:

> **Runners**

People who enter Drop zones, race corrupted Bots to weapons, recover them and protect settlements.

This is useful because it allows normal humans to participate in the same basic resource conflict as the gameplay without turning everyone into superheroes.

The term and profession remain optional.

## 16.4 Aliens remain distant

Strong direction:

The occupiers should rarely need to walk openly through the streets in the present day.

Their victory happened long ago.

Their presence can instead be felt through:
- Drop systems;
- inaccessible structures;
- orbital objects;
- transmissions;
- Reconstruction ruins;
- rare direct interventions.

This preserves scale and mystery.

# 18. PLAYER KNOWLEDGE VS TRUE HISTORY

A central storytelling rule is that the player should not receive the entire bible as exposition.

## 18.1 What ordinary people may know at game start
Possible public knowledge:
- weapon Drops exist;
- Bots once helped control Drops;
- Bots are now dangerous;
- the world suffered a catastrophic historical collapse;
- old ruins and forbidden systems exist.

## 18.2 What ordinary people should NOT know at game start
- Crystala exists;
- EAX-1's abundance came from her;
- weapon production exposed her location;
- aliens are deliberately using Drops to wake her;
- Botfall was deliberate alien sabotage;
- the Crystala awakening countdown is active.

The player should discover these facts through the world and story.

# 19. DAY ZERO — OPENING IMAGE

The current intended opening foundation:

NEWBOT lies discarded in the Scrap region.

Its memory is gone.

It wakes after the Botfall era has already transformed the world.

A weapon-related event/Drop provides an immediate gameplay stimulus.

NEWBOT instinctively recognizes the weapon-management/combat context even though it does not remember its own past.

The opening should teach:
- movement;
- weapon opportunity;
- competition for weapons;
- the danger of Fallen Bots

before explaining cosmic lore.

Guiding principle:

> **The player learns EAX-1 by surviving inside it, not by watching a lore lecture.**

The exact first quest trigger remains **OPEN**.

# 20. STORY DESIGN LAWS

These are important safeguards for all future writing.

### LAW 1 — WORLD BEFORE ROSTER
Do not design history merely to give every playable hero a convenient connection.

### LAW 2 — NEWBOT IS NOT THE CENTER OF HISTORY
NEWBOT can become important after Day Zero, but ancient events did not happen because of it.

### LAW 3 — EACH HERO NEEDS AN INDEPENDENT LIFE
Ask:
> “If NEWBOT never woke up, would this person's history and conflict still make sense?”

If no, the character placement is probably forced.

### LAW 4 — GAMEPLAY SHOULD SUPPORT LORE
Prefer origins that explain why a hero naturally behaves the way its existing mechanic already behaves.

Do not rewrite a good mechanic only to satisfy lore.

### LAW 5 — CRYSTALA IS A KEEPER, NOT AN OMNIPOTENT OWNER
Her restrictions must remain meaningful.

### LAW 6 — DO NOT OVER-EXPLAIN THE ALIENS EARLY
Their name, biology, government and full motivation are not required for Saga 1.

### LAW 7 — WEAPON DROPS MUST REMAIN CENTRAL
Story should deepen the Arsenal loop, not turn the actual combat system into an unrelated minigame between cutscenes.

### LAW 8 — NO GENERIC TOURNAMENT PREMISE
A tournament can exist somewhere in the world later, but it is not the foundational explanation for Arsenal Quest.

### LAW 9 — TITLES MUST BE EARNED OR HISTORICAL
Use:
> Name + the + Identity

The epithet should carry history, reputation, role, tragedy or mystery.

### LAW 10 — KEEP CANON AND BRAINSTORMING SEPARATE
Never silently upgrade a cool suggestion into permanent canon.

# 21. CURRENT TIMELINE v0.1

1. **CRYSTALA'S FLIGHT**  
   Crystala is pursued across the universe while protecting the hidden gems.

2. **THE HIDING**  
   She chooses EAX-1 and embeds her presence into the planet.

3. **THE ABUNDANCE**  
   Crystala energy makes EAX-1 unnaturally resource-rich.

4. **THE DIVIDE**  
   Civilization polarizes into two major powers.

5. **THE ARMS RACE**  
   Both sides mass-produce weapons while never beginning the expected war.

6. **THE SIGNAL**  
   Concentrated Crystala energy in planetary-scale weapon production exposes EAX-1.

7. **THE FALL**  
   Alien forces invade. The two human poles unite and are defeated.

8. **THE DISCOVERY**  
   The invaders learn that destroying human life may destroy access to Crystala.

9. **RECONSTRUCTION**  
   Humanity and society are rebuilt under alien control.

10. **THE DROPS**  
    Captured EAX-1 weapons are circulated back into society to stimulate conflict/resonance.

11. **THE BOT ERA**  
    Humans develop Bots to manage weapon Drops and reduce danger.

12. **BOTFALL**  
    Alien sabotage infects/corrupts the Bot system.

13. **THE AWAKENING**  
    Global weapon conflict rises; Crystala begins to wake.

14. **DAY ZERO**  
    A discarded, uninfected machine opens its eyes in a scrapyard:

    > **NEWBOT**

# 23. NEXT AUTHORING STEP

Do **not** immediately write biographies for all 12 heroes.

Recommended next step:

> Design the playable opening from NEWBOT's first boot through the first clear Quest objective.

That opening should establish Present-Day EAX-1 through action:
- scrapyard;
- first weapon opportunity;
- first corrupted Bot;
- first sign of human civilization;
- first reason NEWBOT must leave the scrapyard.

Only after that objective becomes clear should Quest 01's antagonist/boss be selected.

This prevents the plot from becoming:
> NEWBOT meets Hero A → Hero B → Hero C simply because the roster contains them.
